Prioritize the free custom homepage direction before any build fee as the main offer; launch timing, guarantee, and ongoing support are supporting risk-removal elements.

**Why:** On 2026-09-11 the user tried asymmetric cards, then rejected the result as too SaaS-like and explicitly requested an open editorial two-column composition instead.

**How to apply:** Use an offer statement and CTA on the left, open numbered commitments and dividers on the right. No individual card backgrounds, rounded boxes, shadows, or badges. Keep the first commitment slightly prominent through typography only. Do not extend section guarantee wording to other pages or legal terms.
