name: Well-driller guarantee wording & links
description: Where guarantee trust lines may link and why wording must not be expanded
---

Guarantee mentions on funnel pages (e.g. well-drillers pricing bridge "money-back guarantee" / "free refresh every 2 years") must link to `/pricing` and `/pricing#stay-current-guarantee` — never to the Terms of Service.

**Why:** The ToS explicitly states fees are non-refundable and contains no money-back section. Linking guarantee claims to ToS would contradict it; the guarantee wording lives only on /pricing. Expanding or rephrasing guarantee copy risks creating promises the legal docs don't back.

**How to apply:** When adding guarantee/trust lines anywhere on the site, reuse the exact /pricing wording and link there. Do not invent stronger claims or link legal pages for them.
