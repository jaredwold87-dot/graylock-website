---
name: Promotion dismissal policy
description: Closing a promotion hides its notice but does not withdraw eligibility.
---
Closing the Build-Fee Waiver popup hides that campaign’s notice for the recognized visitor, even if configuration changes. It does not forfeit offer eligibility before the deadline.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose notice-only dismissal rather than withdrawing the offer. Do not use “close this and lose the offer” wording.

**How to apply:** Preserve attribution and normal form access after dismissal. Browser storage or an anonymous assignment identifies the visitor; do not promise cross-device recognition.
