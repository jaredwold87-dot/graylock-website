Treat supplied industry copy as approved wording, not as independent evidence for quotations or policy promises. Verify proof against its public source and policy claims against current authoritative terms before release.

**Why:** A developer-ready industry specification included a refund promise absent from the current Pricing page and a quotation that was not publicly verifiable. Its own conflict rule required linking to current terms or omitting dependent proof rather than publishing unsupported claims.

**How to apply:** Preserve exact approved wording elsewhere. Omit the entire failed proof block with no placeholder, and link away from conflicting policy statements. Do not change global policies or shared forms to make an industry-page specification appear consistent.
