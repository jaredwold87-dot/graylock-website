# Graylock Digital: `/about` Page Redesign Spec

**To:** Replit Build Agent
**From:** Graylock Digital Website Designer
**Task:** Redesign the `/about` page to elevate the visual storytelling, personality, and brand depth. The current page is too generic and corporate. We are shifting to a premium, rugged, founder-led aesthetic inspired by the Montana Knife Company (MKC) About page.

Execute the following layout, content, and visual changes exactly as specified.

---

## 1. Hero Section Overhaul

**Current Issue:** The header is too tall, bland, and lacks visual impact.
**MKC Inspiration:** MKC uses a full-width cinematic video/image hero with a strong overlay and a bold, centered mission statement.

**Required Fixes:**
*   **Reduce Height:** Decrease the hero section minimum height from `100vh` (or whatever it currently is) to `60vh` or `70vh` max on desktop. It should be a strong banner, not a full-page scroll trap.
*   **Visual Background:** Implement a dark, textured background. Use a dark topographic map pattern or a subtle dark forge/sparks texture (similar to the brand wallpapers) with a heavy dark overlay (`rgba(15, 30, 60, 0.8)`).
*   **Typography:** Center the text. Make the H1 massive, distressed, and bold (e.g., using a font like *Tungsten* or *Bebas Neue* if available, or a heavy sans-serif).
    *   **New H1:** `BUILT FOR THE TRADES. BUILT TO WIN.`
    *   **New Subheadline:** `You started your business to do the work — not to manage a website. We build digital assets that work as hard as you do.`
*   **CTA:** Keep the "Book a Discovery Call" button, but style it in the brand's solid orange with sharp, square corners (no pill shapes).

---

## 2. The "4 C's" / Core Philosophy Section

**Current Issue:** The philosophy section ("We Only Work With Businesses...") is a wall of text.
**MKC Inspiration:** MKC breaks their philosophy into "The 4 C's" (Country, Customer, Community, Conservation) using a clean, icon-driven grid.

**Required Fixes:**
*   **Layout:** Create a 4-column grid on desktop (2x2 on mobile).
*   **Content:** Replace the current "Our Philosophy" text with Graylock's 4 Pillars:
    1.  **Craftsmanship:** `Custom builds, no templates. Every pixel is intentional.`
    2.  **Conversion:** `We don't build brochures. We build lead-generation machines.`
    3.  **Compliance:** `Built to federal, state, and industry advertising standards.`
    4.  **Commitment:** `Month-to-month support. We maintain it forever.`
*   **Visuals:** Use stark, high-contrast, minimalist icons for each pillar (e.g., a drafting compass, a target/bullseye, a shield, a handshake).

---

## 3. Founder Bios: The "Origin Story"

**Current Issue:** The bios for Jared and Tim feel like corporate LinkedIn summaries. They lack grit and personality.
**MKC Inspiration:** MKC uses high-contrast, black-and-white editorial portraits paired with first-person, story-driven copy.

**Required Fixes:**
*   **Imagery:** Replace the current founder images with the new MKC-style black-and-white studio portraits (Jared in the hat, Tim without).
*   **Layout:** Use an alternating left/right layout. Jared's photo on the left, text on the right. Tim's photo on the right, text on the left.
*   **Copy Rewrite (Jared):**
    *   *Heading:* `Jared — Co-Founder`
    *   *Body:* `I got tired of watching hardworking local business owners get taken advantage of by flashy agencies selling $20,000 digital brochures that didn't make the phone ring. I built Graylock Digital because the trades deserve better. A great website isn't a luxury for the Fortune 500 — it's the most important tool in your truck. I personally lead every engagement to make sure your site actually does its job: getting you more jobs.`
*   **Copy Rewrite (Tim):**
    *   *Heading:* `Tim — Co-Founder`
    *   *Body:* `Speed and quality usually don't mix in this industry. Agencies take months; cheap freelancers cut corners. I spent years engineering a build system that solves that. We deliver custom, high-converting sites in 7 to 10 days, built entirely in the USA. I oversee the strategy and architecture from kickoff to launch because when our name is on the product, it has to be perfect.`

---

## 4. The "American Made" / Team Section

**Current Issue:** The "U.S.-Based Team" section is functional but lacks emotional resonance.
**MKC Inspiration:** MKC leans heavily into American manufacturing and local jobs with strong, patriotic imagery.

**Required Fixes:**
*   **Visuals:** Replace the small American flag icon with a full-width, high-contrast black-and-white background image of a rugged American landscape or a dark, cinematic American flag texture.
*   **Overlay Text:**
    *   *H2:* `100% U.S.-BASED. ZERO OUTSOURCING.`
    *   *Body:* `Every line of code, every word of copy, and every design decision is made right here in the United States. We are a small American business supporting other hardworking American businesses. No offshore teams. No call centers. Just partners who care about your livelihood as much as you do.`

---

## 5. Timeline / The Graylock Way

**Current Issue:** The "How Graylock Came to Be" and the Process steps are disjointed.
**MKC Inspiration:** MKC uses a clean, horizontal scrolling timeline to tell their story chronologically.

**Required Fixes:**
*   **Layout:** Combine the "Origins" and the "Process" into a single, interactive horizontal timeline (or a vertical stepping-stone layout on mobile).
*   **Steps:**
    1.  **The Problem:** `Small businesses were getting burned by overpriced agencies.`
    2.  **The Solution:** `We engineered a 7-10 day custom build framework.`
    3.  **The Demo:** `We prove it first. You see a custom homepage before paying.`
    4.  **The Launch:** `Your site goes live, optimized for SEO and conversion.`
    5.  **The Partnership:** `We maintain, host, and update it forever.`

---

## 6. Global Styling Updates

*   **Color Palette:** Strictly enforce the brand palette: Pure Black (`#000000`), Dark Charcoal (`#111111`), Brand Orange (`#E8622A`), and Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`). Remove any soft grays or corporate blues.
*   **Typography:** Headings must be uppercase, tight tracking, bold. Body copy should be highly legible but use a modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Roboto).
*   **Texture:** Introduce subtle grit/distress overlays on background sections to break up the flat digital feel.
