SECTION 8 — AUCTIONEER FAQS

FAQ 1: What exactly is the free custom demo?
Answer: It is a custom homepage direction built around your auction business, services, specialties, upcoming-event presentation, and the kinds of opportunities you want more of. It is not a generic template preview, and there is no cost or obligation to review it.

FAQ 2: What happens after I request a demo?
Answer: We set up a short conversation to learn about your auction business, current website, specialties, and goals. Then a Graylock founder walks you through the custom direction and answers the questions that matter to your business.

FAQ 3: Do I have to decide on the call?
Answer: No. The demo exists so you can see something real before deciding whether Graylock is the right fit. There is no obligation to move forward after you review it.

FAQ 4: Why did you contact my auction business?
Answer: We focus on established auctioneers with a real business, a credible specialty, and an online presence we believe can be made more useful. We do not send the same offer to every company.

FAQ 5: Can the website support different kinds of auctions?
Answer: Yes. The website can be structured around the services and event categories that matter to your business, including general auctions, contract sales, benefit events, livestock auctions, estate or equipment sales, real-estate auctions, and other specialties you offer.

FAQ 6: Can the website connect people to our current bidding or registration platform?
Answer: We can plan clear links, calls to action, and approved integrations around the platform you use. Any specific third-party platform integration is confirmed during scoping so the experience and cost are clear before work begins.

FAQ 7: Will the site help us get found on Google?
Answer: We build around clear auction services, specialties, locations, event types, local-search fundamentals, and content structure so search engines and prospects can better understand what you do. No agency can ethically guarantee a specific ranking.

FAQ 8: How do seller and event requests reach us?
Answer: We configure clear call actions and request forms so the details are sent directly to the email inbox or CRM your business designates.

FAQ 9: Can we keep new auction information current?
Answer: Yes. Your monthly plan includes a defined update allowance, and we can help keep key website information current. More extensive event-management or integration work is scoped clearly before work begins.

FAQ 10: What does it mean that the build fee may be waived?
Answer: Graylock is currently waiving the one-time website build fee for qualified auctioneer businesses it chooses to work with. We confirm whether the offer applies before you commit to a full build.
