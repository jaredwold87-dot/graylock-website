# Replit Prompt: Simplify the Real-Estate Hero Trust Bar

On the real-estate landing page, simplify the bottom of the hero section. The current design has **two stacked horizontal information bars** beneath the hero content, creating too much dense copy and making the end of the hero feel cluttered.

## Objective

Remove the redundant feature row and keep **one clean, premium trust bar** as the final visual element of the hero. The hero should end with a short, memorable value proposition - not a technical feature list.

## Required Changes

### 1. Remove the upper feature row entirely

Delete the row that currently contains these four items:

- Custom brand + local-market pages
- IDX search when eligible
- Seller valuation + contact capture
- MLS-aware setup + ongoing support

Do not replace this row with another row in the hero. These are product features and should be explained later on the page in a dedicated capabilities, IDX, or feature section.

### 2. Keep only one dark trust bar at the bottom of the hero

Retain the lower black/dark horizontal bar, but reduce it from four claims to **three equally spaced items**.

Use the existing Graylock orange line icons, white/very-light text, dark background, and the same premium visual styling. Keep the bar visually quiet, clean, and easy to scan.

### 3. Replace the current trust-bar copy with exactly these three items

| Position | Suggested icon | Exact copy |
|---|---|---|
| Left | Shield / protected-market icon | **One brokerage per local market** |
| Center | Spark / linked-mark / custom-direction icon | **Free custom homepage direction** |
| Right | Globe / domain icon | **Built on your domain. Yours to keep.** |

Use title case or sentence case consistently with the rest of the page, but do not add supporting subtext below these lines.

## Design Requirements

- Use a three-column grid on desktop with the items evenly distributed across the container.
- On mobile, stack the three items vertically or use a clean one-column layout with comfortable spacing. Do not let the text become cramped or wrap awkwardly.
- Keep a subtle top border/divider between the hero content and the trust bar if one already exists.
- The trust bar should be noticeably shorter and calmer than its current state.
- Preserve the dark Graylock visual language: black or charcoal background, orange icons, white text, restrained borders.
- Do not add animations, extra badges, logos, fine print, or secondary feature copy in this bar.

## Move These Items Out of the Hero

The following details are valuable but too technical or too dense for the hero. Move them to the relevant lower-page sections if they are not already covered there:

| Current item | Recommended destination |
|---|---|
| Elm Street IDX Broker Developer Partner | IDX / MLS integrations section, with a short explanation of eligibility and approved data partners. |
| IDX search when eligible | IDX capabilities section. |
| Seller valuation + contact capture | Lead-generation or seller-tools section. |
| MLS-aware setup + ongoing support | IDX/setup/process section. |
| Month-to-month support + refreshes while subscribed | Pricing, support, or ongoing partnership section. |

## Quality Check Before Completing

Confirm the final hero has only:

1. The primary real-estate headline and supporting hero content.
2. The visual/mockup portion of the hero.
3. One single three-item trust bar at the bottom.

The page should feel materially less crowded. The hero’s job is to communicate the primary promise; lower sections can explain technical integrations, lead tools, and support mechanics.
